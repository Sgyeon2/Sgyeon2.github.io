About

Putting language models to work on real-world decisions.

I’m an M.S. student in Information Systems at Korea University. I’m interested in using language models to tackle real-world challenges — combining NLP techniques with rigorous data science methodology to build frameworks that improve decision-making and user experience.

My latest paper tackles data scarcity in cybersecurity through LLM-based data augmentation. A reinforcement learning policy chooses a persuasion principle — such as authority or social proof — for each smishing (SMS phishing) message and builds it into the LLM prompt, so the generated samples stay faithful to the original while adding useful diversity. In a real-world smishing detection setting with very little training data, this improved F1 by 2.5–8.1 points over state-of-the-art LLM-based augmentation methods.

Outside research, I enjoy competitive machine learning — most recently placing 6th at the LG Aimers 9th hackathon.

Pitch Control Grade Prediction

Sep 2026
LG Aimers 9th Offline Hackathon

Predicted KBO pitch control grade probabilities (Shadow / Heart / Failure) from pre-pitch information only, scored by row-level Brier score and pitcher-level Kendall’s τb ranking.

6th
Final rank
70.69
Total score (baseline 68.53)
72.24
Pitcher ranking (from 64.59)
How I approached it
  • Reconstructed 1.2M training labels to detect an umpiring regime shift and select training seasons.
  • Leakage-free pitcher-history features normalized against season × league averages; LightGBM sub-model for rookies.
  • Resolved a Simpson’s paradox between row- and pitcher-level objectives with a Brier-cost-minimizing probability shift.

25th

How I approached it
  • Domain-rule post-processing that zeroes probability for cycles without embryo transfer.
  • XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost soft-voting ensemble blended 0.8 : 0.2 with TabM (k=32, 5 seeds).
  • 30-fold repeated stratified CV with 95% CIs; private score exceeded CV — no leaderboard overfitting.

Defect Detection in Automotive Display Manufacturing

Aug 2024
LG Aimers 5th Online Hackathon

Classified defective (AbNormal) products from multi-sensor process data on an automotive display production line, where defects are a small minority of the data.

0.1823
Leaderboard F1
0.24
Validation F1 (defective class)
~6%
Defective share of data
How I approached it
  • Work-order risk feature: binned each work order’s defect rate into high / normal / low risk categories as a model input.
  • Handled class imbalance with stratified splits, XGBoost scale_pos_weight, and a decision threshold tuned for defective-class F1.
  • Weighted soft-voting ensemble of XGBoost, RandomForest, CatBoost, and LightGBM with depth limits and regularization to curb overfitting.
