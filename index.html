I’m an M.S. student in Information Systems at Korea University, advised by Prof. Kyuhan Lee.

My latest paper, currently under review, tackles data scarcity in cybersecurity through LLM-based data augmentation. A reinforcement learning policy chooses a persuasion principle, such as authority or social proof, for each smishing (SMS phishing) message and builds it into the LLM prompt, so the generated samples stay faithful to the original while adding useful diversity. In a real-world smishing detection setting with very little training data, this improved F1 by 2.5–8.1 points over state-of-the-art LLM-based augmentation methods.

Outside research, I enjoy machine learning competitions; write-ups are under Projects.

Projects

Pitch Control Grade Prediction

Sep 2026
LG Aimers 9th Offline Hackathon

Predicted KBO pitch control grade probabilities (Shadow / Heart / Failure) from pre-pitch information only, scored by row-level Brier score and pitcher-level Kendall’s τb ranking.

6th place. Total score 70.69 (baseline 68.53); pitcher ranking score 72.24, up from 64.59.

  • Reconstructed 1.2M training labels to detect an umpiring regime shift and select training seasons.
  • Leakage-free pitcher-history features normalized against season × league averages; LightGBM sub-model for rookies.
  • Resolved a Simpson’s paradox between row- and pitcher-level objectives with a Brier-cost-minimizing probability shift.

Pregnancy Success Prediction for Infertility Patients

Feb 2025
LG Aimers 6th Online Hackathon

Binary classifier predicting pregnancy success from clinical and treatment-history data, evaluated by ROC-AUC.

25th place (top 4%). Private ROC-AUC 0.7422, against 0.7406 in 30-fold CV.

  • Domain-rule post-processing that zeroes probability for cycles without embryo transfer.
  • XGBoost / LightGBM / CatBoost soft-voting ensemble blended 0.8 : 0.2 with TabM (k=32, 5 seeds).
  • 30-fold repeated stratified CV with 95% CIs; the private score came in above CV, so the leaderboard was not overfit.

Defect Detection in Automotive Display Manufacturing

Aug 2024
LG Aimers 5th Online Hackathon

Classified defective (AbNormal) products from multi-sensor process data on an automotive display production line, where defective products make up about 6% of the data.

Leaderboard F1 0.1823; validation F1 0.24 on the defective class.

  • Work-order risk feature: binned each work order’s defect rate into high / normal / low risk categories as a model input.
  • Handled class imbalance with stratified splits, XGBoost scale_pos_weight, and a decision threshold tuned for defective-class F1.
  • Weighted soft-voting ensemble of XGBoost, RandomForest, CatBoost, and LightGBM with depth limits and regularization to curb overfitting.
